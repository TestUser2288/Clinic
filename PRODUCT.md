# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React + TypeScript single-page app, installable as a PWA. Mobile-first; most visitors arrive on phones.

## Users

Medical doctors and residents preparing a medical thesis (clinical, epidemiological, observational or experimental studies). Strong in medicine, usually not in statistics, short on time between hospital duties and night shifts. They browse on their phone, often late.

## Product Purpose

Turn a doctor's thesis problem into a quote request for a freelance statistician. The visitor understands the offer, sees the process and the confidentiality promise, and messages on WhatsApp (pre-filled). Success = a WhatsApp quote request.

## Positioning

A statistical *consultation*, not a portfolio: the site speaks about the doctor's study and uses the codes of phone health apps (bottom tabs, cards, one-tap WhatsApp). Quote-only, tailored per thesis.

## Operating Context

- Doctor on a phone, between duties or at night; dark mode matters.
- Contact is WhatsApp-first (messages and voice notes); email is secondary. No phone calls, no booking system, no social media.
- Quote returned on WhatsApp (or email) within 24–48 h.
- Typical needs: sample size, questionnaire/database structuring, data cleaning, descriptive stats, comparison tests, odds ratios / logistic regression, survival analysis, interpretation, thesis committee questions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Languages: French default, English toggle, remembered between visits. Medical/statistical terms accurate in both.
- 3 bottom tabs: Accueil (Home), Confiance (Proof), Devis (Contact). The Diagnostic intake was removed at the client's request (2026-10-18); do not reintroduce it.
- No "pas d'appel téléphonique" note on the contact screen (removed at the client's request).
- Extras: "Quel test statistique ?" mini-tool and a simple sample-size helper, linked from Home. Dark mode follows the system setting.
- No public prices anywhere.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Stat Clinic** (working brand).
- Reference image supplied by the client is binding: the screens should look like it (photo hero with white headline, pill buttons, three photo cards, mint trust strip, white cards, numbered timeline, mint info cards, WhatsApp-green contact badge). An earlier "elevated" reinterpretation was rejected by the client as too far from the image.
- Photos: free Unsplash stock, no faces (see PHOTO-CREDITS.md).
- Tone: warm, direct, jargon-free, colleague-to-colleague; respectful of the doctor's expertise.

## Evidence on Hand

- Credentials: ESSAI engineering degree; tools R, Python, SPSS.
- No real photo, testimonials, or anonymised case studies yet. Testimonials are deliberately left out of the site for now; do not fabricate any. Before/after visuals use clearly synthetic demo data.

## Product Principles

1. Talk about the doctor's study, never about the statistician first.
2. Every step should lower a fear: confidentiality, medical understanding, defendable results, honesty, cost/time.
3. Fewer taps to a structured WhatsApp request beats more content.
4. Honesty is a feature: never promise results, never show invented proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Thumb-reachable primary actions, large tap targets, readable at night (dark mode), respects reduced motion, bilingual FR/EN.
